# How to organise events in general

- Set event dates early in the semester (ideally before semester starts)
- once all events are set, send an event announcement Email + WhatsApp

## General
### Location
- Email people to get approval for the rooms
	- see https://github.com/LST-Student-Council-Saarland/protocols/blob/main/guides/fachschaft/Checkliste%20f%C3%BCr%20die%20Fachschaft.pdf
- buildings are closed at night → we can borrow a guest key card for C7.1/2./3./4. from Diana (go to Amira for this)

### Announcement
- Write an announcement 2 weeks (?) before, then a reminder:
	- Email + WhatsApp
- put up posters on C.7.2. glass front, student chill room, and A.2.2. student room and stairs
- tell people about it personally

### Food & Drinks
- apply for Asta-Money (at least 2 weeks before the event!!!)
	- https://asta.uni-saarland.de/fachschaften-antraege/
- keep receipts
- don't keep perishable food or opened food in the SC rooms!
- make sure we have enough plates, cups, cleaning supplies etc

### Equipment
- we can borrow benches from Asta: (Summer Party)
	- https://asta.uni-saarland.de/fachschaften-antraege/

## Event Types
- Semester Start Breakfast
- Game Night
- Winter Party
- ESC finals watch party
- Open Mic Night
- Summer Party (-> invite everyone in the department + friends of lst!)
