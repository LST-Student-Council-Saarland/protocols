## General
### Location
- usually we ask Khg (events are generally possible on all days except Thursdays)
	- they have a lot of games
	- we should move the tables back after, so it's good if we take a picture/take notes before
- usually a Wednesday, 6pm

## Food
### Pizza Order
- (everyone pays for their own pizza)
- decide on a place before
- convenient if someone has Liferando (or similar) and PayPal
- make sure someone has enough money on them ( we can no longer withdraw money on campus) or can pay by credit card
	- (else we just have everyone give us cash but this is less stressful)
- do an announcement early in the event, let everyone put their order on a list

### Snacks and Drinks:
- we usually buy some softdrinks (sprite, juices, coke) and some sparkling water
- some snacks: chips, crackers, nuts, ...
- khg has a kitchen with glasses and plates, and a dishwasher
- also put out jugs with tap water

### Cleanup
- start the dishwasher early enough
- sweep the floor in the end
- big trash (e.g. pizza boxes) in a big plastic bag, put them on the collection spot outside
